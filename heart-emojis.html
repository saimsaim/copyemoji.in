Meanings & When to Use

Red heart ❤️ is romance and deep love. Orange, yellow, green and blue hearts are friendly and safe for friends — yellow is Snapchat-best-friends energy, green is healthy/K-pop support, blue is trust and loyalty. Purple 💜 signals sensitive love and fandom pride. Black 🖤 and white 🤍 dominate aesthetic Instagram bios and Discord statuses. Pink 🩷 is cute-crush and caring. Broken 💔 is heartbreak, mending ❤️‍🩹 is healing. Beating 💓 and growing 💗 show excitement, sparkling 💖 and revolving 💞 show playful affection. Arrow 💘 is crush/valentine, ribbon 💝 is gifting.

Rule of thumb: 1 heart for friends, stacked hearts 💕💞 for excitement, never send ❤️ to a new work contact — use 💙 or 🤍 instead. On WhatsApp pair hearts with no more than 1-2 per message or the tone looks sarcastic. Full breakdowns: Red Heart, Broken Heart, Beating Heart.

Hearts for Instagram Bio & Discord

Bio combos that always render: 🤍 🖤, ♡ ♥, ❤️‍🔥 single, 💔 healing → ❤️‍🩹. Discord-safe: all Unicode hearts work in status, nicknames and About Me. Custom server emoji do not paste cross-server — stick to this grid. More symbols: Discord Symbols, Aesthetic Emojis, Symbols category.
