Emoji Meaning Slang: What They Really Mean in Chat

Dictionary definitions lie. In real texting, 🫯 is burnout, 🪫 is “my social battery is dead”, 📲 is “call me / check DMs”, 🔒 is locked-in or taken, ♨️ is a steamy hint, 🫣 is cringe-watching, 👹 is demon-mode joking, 🥭 is a sweet thirst-trap. The words around the emoji decide everything — lone 👀 is shady, 👀 + 😍 is interested, 💀 alone is “I’m dead laughing”.

From a guy vs from a girl? Same emoji, different temperature. Guys use 🔒🙃🤡 for low-effort banter and flirting without typing a paragraph. Girls use 🥺😍🫠 to tune emotion — warm, ironic or deliberately vague. Late-night “hey 🫣” hits different than midday “thanks 🫣”. Frequently Asked Questions

What does 🫯 mean in text?

Exhausted or burnout — dead-inside humour. Tap it above to copy.

What does 📲 mean on Instagram slang?

Call me / check DMs / link in bio. Works in bios and captions.

What does 🔒 mean from a guy?

Locked in, taken or keep this private — commitment or secrecy.

What does ♨ mean in text from a guy?

A steamy hint — attraction or hot gossip. Girls use it for spa-day too.
